## Goal
You're building a single full-screen interactive UI piece: **a dark glass tab bar where the active tab sits under a soft
beam of light**. In the middle of a near-black stage with film grain lies a frosted glass bar (62 px tall, fully
rounded) with five tabs, each a Lucide icon and a label in Geist Medium: Overview, Projects, Library, Team, Settings.
The bar has a fine light rim, tinted mint at its left end and lilac at its right. The active tab sits in a raised glass
pill and is lit: its label near-white, its icon a cool mint with a soft mint glow. Above it hangs a beam of light: a
cone made of a soft body, nine rays that breathe at their own pace, a bright core thread and dust motes drifting up
through it; it pools as a soft light on the floor under the bar, runs as a thin light line along the bar's top rim, and
falls into the pill from its top edge.
Hover a tab and a faint glass pill fades in under the pointer (and follows softly if you move to another tab). Click a
tab and the active pill travels there on springs: one spring per edge, the edge in front stiffer, so the pill stretches
as it goes and settles back. While the pill moves fast the beam focuses into a thin bright thread, and follows the pill
with its own lag, leaning into it; when the pill slows the beam blooms back into a cone with a pulse of light on the
pill and the floor. Labels light up as the pill passes over them. Left alone for 4 s, a demo hand cursor takes over and
plays a 10.6-second loop (Projects, Team, Library, Settings, Overview); it also plays from the start.
The design is a 1080 × 1080 stage that scales to fit the window (it fills the window's shorter side) and stays centred;
the scene on it (bar, beam, floor, cursor) is drawn at 1.45× around the stage's centre, and the backdrop and grain run
on past the stage to the window's edges. There are no images: CSS, one canvas for the beam, inline SVG icons, Geist
from Google Fonts. Match everything below exactly: layout, colours, layers and every motion number. Keep the copy as
given; the reader swaps in their own afterwards.

### 1. Page, stage, scene and grain
- `.page` (the `<main>`) is fixed over the whole window, isolated (its own blending group) and paints the backdrop: an
  elliptical light 648 × 486 design px (radii), centred horizontally and 21.6 design px below the window's centre,
  fading `#121214` → `#08080a` (60%) → `#040405`. Because it's placed in stage units from the window's centre, inside
  the stage it is exactly `radial-gradient(60% 45% at 50% 52%, …)` of a 1080 square, and outside it (wide or tall
  windows) it runs on to the window's edges with no seam.
- `.stage` is the 1080 × 1080 square centred in the window; it paints the same backdrop again and does **not** clip (on
  a tall window the beam's top, which starts above the stage, stays visible).
- `.scene` fills the stage and is scaled `1.45` around (540, 540). It is `visibility: hidden` until the script has
  drawn its first frame and set `data-ready` on it.
- `.grain` covers the window: it's a child of the stage reaching 2000 design px past it on every side (2000 is a
  multiple of the 200-px tile, so the tiles stay lined up with the stage; that covers windows up to about 4.7:1),
  `opacity: 0.09`, `mix-blend-mode: overlay`.

### 2. The beam and the floor light

## Motion
All times in seconds. The state is stepped once per animation frame with `T` = the frame timestamp in seconds (`now /
1000`, straight from `requestAnimationFrame`); the demo loop counts from the first frame. The frame step is the time
since the last frame, capped at 0.04 s (1/60 s on the first frame). Springs are stepped with semi-implicit Euler
(velocity first, then position), once per frame, so they are frame-rate dependent: that's intended.

| Behaviour | Trigger | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Demo | from the start, and whenever nobody has hovered, clicked or pressed a key for 4 s | loop phase `t = (T − start) % 10.6`. The cursor rests at scene (760, 700) until 0.6 s, then glides to a tab (62% along it, y 548) and clicks it: Projects 0.6 → click 1.3, Team 2.6 → 3.2, Library 4.6 → 5.1, Settings 6.5 → 7.0, Overview 8.6 → 9.1. It eases at rate 5.5/s, so it glides past other tabs on the way (they show the hover pill too). A click fires once within 0.2 s of its time and the cursor shows pressed for 0.16 s. While the demo plays, the hovered tab is the one under its fingertip (scene y between 512 and 570) |
| Hand-back | 4 s after the last hover, click or key | the demo resumes at its own phase (it may glide or click at once). Only entering a tab counts as a hover: a pointer resting on a tab for 4 s is taken over by the demo too (its cursor and hover replace yours), and moving the pointer off a tab doesn't count as input. Keep that |
| Hover | the pointer enters a tab | that tab is hovered (and the demo holds off); leaving it un-hovers. The hover pill shows only on a hovered tab that isn't active |
| Select | click (or Enter / Space) on a tab; ←/→ (wrapping), Home, End in the tab list | the active tab changes (and focus follows the arrow keys); the pill's leading edge is the side it moves towards |
| Active pill | the active tab | two springs, left and right edge. Stiffness eases (rate 6/s) towards 230 for the leading edge and 150 for the trailing one (both start at 300), damping stays critical |
| Beam focus | the pill's edge speed | above 35 design px/s (both edges together) the focus springs to 0.12 (a thin thread), else to 1; it may overshoot to 1.25 |
| Bloom | the focus rises past 0.75 | a pulse of 1 that decays to 12% per second; it can fire again once the focus has dropped below 0.4 |
| Beam position | the pill's centre | follows at rate 9/s; its lean (`skewX`) eases at 6/s towards −0.05° per px of lag, clamped to ±10° |
| Tab light | each frame | each tab's label and icon light up as the pill covers them and stay lit while active; a hovered tab lights to 0.35 |

**Script part 1.** Every constant, the beam's drawing, the state and its step (the hard bit), and the function that
writes each frame to the DOM. The order of the calculations matters: the pill moves first, then the hover pill, then
the focus from the pill's speed, then the beam follows the pill's new centre, then the tabs' light. Use it exactly as
given. How the animation reaches the page: the page is rendered once and nothing is re-rendered; `frame()` steps the
state and writes the result straight to the DOM, skipping every style write whose value hasn't changed. Positions and
sizes go out as unitless custom properties in design px (`--x`, `--w`, `--y`) that the CSS multiplies by `--u`;
opacity, `color`, `filter`, `background`, `transform` and `display` as style properties; `aria-selected`, `tabindex`
and `data-ready` as attributes; and the beam on its canvas. The rays' widths, strengths, phases and speeds and the
dust's positions, sizes, speeds and phases are random, picked once per page load with `Math.random()` in the order the
code gives (dust first, then rays); don't seed them. Several alpha strings are written at full precision and some with
`toFixed(3)`: keep each as given.
```js
// ---- Constants ----
// Design px of the 1080 × 1080 stage (before the scene's 1.45× scale); times in seconds. Springs have stiffness k and
// damping c (per unit mass) and are stepped once per animation frame with semi-implicit Euler.
const clamp = (v, a = 0, b = 1) => Math.min(b, Math.max(a, v));
const lerp = (a, b, u) => a + (b - a) * u;

const CENTER = 540; // the stage's centre, both axes; the bar is centred on it
const TAB_START = 6, TAB_GAP = 2, BAR_END = 4; // first tab's x in the bar, gap after each tab, extra after the last gap
const BEAM_W = 360, BEAM_H = 400; // the beam canvas: bitmap size and design size alike
// The demo cursor: where it rests, the y it points at on the bar, how far along a tab it points (0–1), the band (stage
// y) in which it counts as over the bar, and its fingertip inside the 22 × 24 drawing.
const CURSOR_REST_X = 760, CURSOR_REST_Y = 700, CURSOR_ON_BAR_Y = 548, CURSOR_ON_TAB = 0.62;
const CURSOR_HIT_TOP = 512, CURSOR_HIT_BOTTOM = 570, CURSOR_TIP_X = 8, CURSOR_TIP_Y = 4;

const DT_FIRST = 1 / 60, DT_MAX = 0.04; // the first frame's step, and the longest step a slow frame may take
// The demo: [time in the loop, tab, click]. The cursor glides to a tab, then clicks it.
const DEMO = [[0.6, 1, 0], [1.3, 1, 1], [2.6, 3, 0], [3.2, 3, 1], [4.6, 2, 0], [5.1, 2, 1], [6.5, 4, 0], [7.0, 4, 1], [8.6, 0, 0], [9.1, 0, 1]];
const LOOP = 10.6;
const LOOP_REARM = 0.05, CLICK_WINDOW = 0.2, PRESS = 0.16, CURSOR_PRESSED = 0.9; // re-arm window, click window, press time, press scale
const CURSOR_RATE = 5.5; // the cursor eases towards its target at this rate (per s)
const HAND_BACK = 4; // a hover, click or key press holds the demo off this long

// The active pill: one spring per edge. The edge in front is stiffer, so it runs ahead and the pill stretches, then the
// trailing edge catches up. Both start at stiffness PILL_K0 and ease to their values at PILL_K_RATE; damping stays
// critical (c = 2√k × PILL_DAMPING).
const PILL_K0 = 300, PILL_K_LEAD = 230, PILL_K_TRAIL = 150, PILL_K_RATE = 6, PILL_DAMPING = 1.0;
const HOVER_K = 420, HOVER_C = 36, FADE_K = 200, FADE_C = 26; // the hover pill's edges, and its fade

// The beam's focus (1 = open cone, lower = thin thread): when the pill's edges move faster than SPEED_THIN together
// (design px/s) it springs to FOCUS_THIN, otherwise back to 1, and may overshoot to FOCUS_MAX. Past BLOOM on the way
// up the beam blooms (a pulse that decays to PULSE_DECAY per second); it can bloom again once it has dropped under REARM.
const SPEED_THIN = 35, FOCUS_THIN = 0.12, FOCUS_K = 90, FOCUS_C = 17, FOCUS_MAX = 1.25;
const BLOOM = 0.75, REARM = 0.4, PULSE_DECAY = 0.12;
// The beam follows the pill's centre at FOLLOW_RATE and leans into its lag (deg per design px, up to TILT_MAX).
const FOLLOW_RATE = 9, TILT_RATE = 6, TILT = -0.05, TILT_MAX = 10;
// What the focus does to the beam's opacity, the rim light's width (× the pill's width) and its opacity.
const BEAM_OPACITY = [0.45, 1], RIM_WIDTH = [0.3, 0.9], RIM_OPACITY = 0.35, RIM_OPACITY_GAIN = 0.65;
// The pool of light on the floor (× the pill's width; alpha from the focus and the pulse), and the light in the pill.
const FLOOR_LEFT = 0.6, FLOOR_WIDTH = 1.2, FLOOR_ALPHA = 0.12, FLOOR_FOCUS = 0.14, FLOOR_PULSE = 0.2;
const CAUSTIC_FOCUS = 0.1, CAUSTIC_PULSE = 0.18;
// Tabs light up as the pill passes over them and stay lit while active (rate per s). A tab is covered when the pill's
// centre is within COVER_SPAN of its width from its own centre; covering lights it to COVER at most, a hover to
// HOVER_LIT. The icon takes its own colour above ICON_ON and glows above GLOW_ON (GLOW px, GLOW_ALPHA).
const LIT_RATE = 9, COVER_SPAN = 0.7, COVER = 0.6, HOVER_LIT = 0.35, ICON_ON = 0.02, GLOW_ON = 0.05, GLOW = 7, GLOW_ALPHA = 0.55;

// The colours written every frame (channels), and the tabs' font and the wait for it.
const TAB_DIM = [92, 92, 100], TAB_LIT = [246, 247, 246], ICON_LIT = [196, 255, 232];
const ICON_GLOW = "150,255,220", FLOOR = "190,255,236", CAUSTIC = "215,255,244";
const FONT = '500 15.5px "Geist"', FONT_WAIT = 3000;
const REDUCED = "(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)";

// ---- Beam ----
// Drawn on the 360 × 400 canvas every frame: a soft body, nine rays that breathe at their own pace, a bright core
// thread and 46 motes of dust drifting up. `focus` 1 is the open cone over a resting pill; towards 0 it narrows to the
// thread. `pulse` is the flash on arrival. The rays and the dust are random, picked once per page load.
// Dust: position across and up the cone (0–1), radius (px), rise speed (cone heights per s) and phase.
const DUST = Array.from({ length: 46 }, () => ({ x: Math.random(), y: Math.random(), s: 0.4 + Math.random() * 1.3, v: 0.03 + Math.random() * 0.08, p: Math.random() * 6 }));
// Rays: offset across the cone (−1 to 1), width (× the cone's foot), strength, phase and breathing speed.
const RAYS = Array.from({ length: 9 }, (_, i) => ({ o: (i - 4) / 4, w: 0.08 + Math.random() * 0.12, a: 0.25 + Math.random() * 0.5, p: Math.random() * 6, s: 0.6 + Math.random() * 0.8 }));

// T the clock (s), w the active pill's width (the open cone's foot is 95% of it), focus (clamped to 0–1 here), pulse 1 → 0.
function drawBeam(g, T, w, focus, pulse) {
  const W = BEAM_W, H = BEAM_H;
  g.clearRect(0, 0, W, H);
  const f = clamp(focus);
  const top = 4 + 6 * f, bot = lerp(14, w * 0.95, f);
  g.globalCompositeOperation = "lighter";
  // a trapezoid from the top edge (half-width half0) to the bottom edge (half1), brightest at the foot
  const body = (half0, half1, alpha) => {
    const gr = g.createLinearGradient(0, 0, 0, H);
    gr.addColorStop(0, "rgba(220,255,246,0)");
    gr.addColorStop(0.45, `rgba(220,255,246,${alpha * 0.25})`);
    gr.addColorStop(0.92, `rgba(225,255,248,${alpha})`);
    gr.addColorStop(1, `rgba(240,255,252,${alpha * 1.2})`);
    g.fillStyle = gr;
    g.beginPath();
    g.moveTo(W / 2 - half0, 0);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 + half0, 0);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 + half1, H);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 - half1, H);
    g.closePath();
    g.fill();
  };
  body(top / 2, bot / 2, 0.07 + 0.05 * pulse);
  // each ray breathes at its own pace
  RAYS.forEach((r) => {
    const br = 0.55 + 0.45 * Math.sin(T * r.s + r.p);
    const cx = r.o * bot * 0.36;
    const hw = bot * r.w * 0.5;
    const gr = g.createLinearGradient(0, 0, 0, H);
    gr.addColorStop(0, "rgba(230,255,250,0)");
    gr.addColorStop(0.6, `rgba(230,255,250,${0.04 * r.a * br * f})`);
    gr.addColorStop(1, `rgba(235,255,252,${(0.14 * r.a * br * f + 0.1 * pulse).toFixed(3)})`);
    g.fillStyle = gr;
    g.beginPath();
    g.moveTo(W / 2 + cx * 0.05 - 1, 0);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 + cx * 0.05 + 1, 0);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 + cx + hw, H);
    g.lineTo(W / 2 + cx - hw, H);
    g.closePath();
    g.fill();
  });
  // the bright core thread: what stays when the beam focuses while the pill moves
  body(1, lerp(3, bot * 0.14, f), 0.16 + 0.12 * (1 - f) + 0.15 * pulse);
  g.globalCompositeOperation = "source-over";
  DUST.forEach((d) => {
    const yy = (((d.y - T * d.v) % 1) + 1) % 1, half = lerp(top, bot, yy) / 2, xx = W / 2 + (d.x - 0.5) * 2 * half * 0.9 + Math.sin(T + d.p) * 3;
    g.fillStyle = `rgba(235,255,250,${((0.12 + 0.45 * yy) * (0.5 + 0.5 * Math.sin(T * 2 + d.p)) * f).toFixed(3)})`;
    g.beginPath();
    g.arc(xx, yy * H, d.s, 0, Math.PI * 2);
    g.fill();
  });
}

// ---- State ----
// Stepped once per animation frame. Positions are design px: tab and pill x along the bar, the beam and the cursor in
// stage x.
const spring = (k, c) => ({ x: 0, v: 0, k, c });
const pull = (sp, target, dt) => { sp.v += (sp.k * (target - sp.x) - sp.c * sp.v) * dt; sp.x += sp.v * dt; }; // one semi-implicit Euler step
const snap = (sp, x) => { sp.x = x; sp.v = 0; };

const s = {
  X: [], Wd: [], BW: 0, // each tab's left edge and width along the bar, and the bar's width (set by layout)
  L: spring(PILL_K0, 0), R: spring(PILL_K0, 0), // the active pill's two edges (damping is set from the stiffness every frame)
  HL: spring(HOVER_K, HOVER_C), HR: spring(HOVER_K, HOVER_C), HO: spring(FADE_K, FADE_C), // the hover pill's edges and its opacity
  FO: { x: 1, v: 0 }, // the beam's focus
  BM: 0, SK: 0, // the beam's x on the stage and its tilt (deg)
  bloomed: true, pulse: 0, // the arrival pulse
  act: 0, hov: -1, // active tab, hovered tab (−1 for none)
  dirK: 0, // which way the pill last set off (1 right, −1 left, 0 not yet)
  lit: [], // how lit each tab is
  user: false, lastUser: -9, // whether someone is using it, and when they last did (s, performance.now() clock)
  start: null, last: null, beamT: 0, // the first frame's time, the last frame's, the time the beam is drawn at
  demoK: -1, cx: CURSOR_REST_X, cy: CURSOR_REST_Y, demo: true, down: false, // the last click the demo fired, its cursor, whether it shows, whether pressed
};

/** The bar's left edge on the stage. */
const barLeft = () => CENTER - s.BW / 2;

/** Lays the tabs out from their widths (design px), left to right with a 2-px gap. */
function layout(widths) {
  let x = TAB_START;
  s.X = [];
  s.Wd = [];
  widths.forEach((w) => {
    s.X.push(x);
    s.Wd.push(w);
    x += w + TAB_GAP;
  });
  s.BW = x + BAR_END;
  s.lit = widths.map((_, i) => (i === s.act ? 1 : 0));
}

function select(i) {
  if (i === s.act) return;
  s.dirK = i > s.act ? 1 : -1;
  s.act = i;
}

/** Someone hovered, clicked or pressed a key: the demo holds off for HAND_BACK seconds. `now` is performance.now() (ms). */
function touch(now) {
  s.user = true;
  s.lastUser = now / 1000;
}

/** One frame at time `now` (ms, the animation frame's timestamp). */
function step(now, reduced) {
  const T = now / 1000;
  if (s.start === null) {
    s.start = T;
    s.beamT = T;
    s.L.x = s.X[0];
    s.R.x = s.X[0] + s.Wd[0];
    s.BM = barLeft() + s.X[0] + s.Wd[0] / 2;
    s.HL.x = s.L.x;
    s.HR.x = s.R.x;
  }
  const dt = s.last === null ? DT_FIRST : Math.min(DT_MAX, T - s.last);
  s.last = T;
  // with reduced motion the rays and the dust hold still
  if (!reduced) s.beamT = T;

  // the demo: when nobody has touched it for HAND_BACK seconds, its cursor glides to each tab and clicks it
  let tx = s.cx, ty = s.cy;
  s.down = false;
  s.demo = !reduced && (!s.user || T - s.lastUser > HAND_BACK);
  if (s.demo) {
    s.user = false;
    const t = (T - s.start) % LOOP;
    let target = null;
    for (let k = 0; k < DEMO.length; k++) {
      const [tt, i, c] = DEMO[k];
      if (t >= tt) {
        target = i;
        if (c && s.demoK !== k && t < tt + CLICK_WINDOW) {
          s.demoK = k;
          select(i);
        }
      }
    }
    if (t < LOOP_REARM) s.demoK = -1;
    if (target !== null) {
      tx = barLeft() + s.X[target] + s.Wd[target] * CURSOR_ON_TAB;
      ty = CURSOR_ON_BAR_Y;
    } else {
      tx = CURSOR_REST_X;
      ty = CURSOR_REST_Y;
    }
    s.down = DEMO.some((d) => d[2] && t >= d[0] && t < d[0] + PRESS);
    s.cx += (tx - s.cx) * Math.min(1, dt * CURSOR_RATE);
    s.cy += (ty - s.cy) * Math.min(1, dt * CURSOR_RATE);
    const bx = s.cx - barLeft();
    s.hov = -1;
    if (s.cy > CURSOR_HIT_TOP && s.cy < CURSOR_HIT_BOTTOM)
      s.X.forEach((x, i) => {
        if (bx >= x && bx <= x + s.Wd[i]) s.hov = i;
      });
  }

  // the active pill: the edge in front is stiffer, so it runs ahead and the pill stretches
  const { L, R, HL, HR, HO, FO } = s;
  const lead = s.dirK > 0 ? "R" : "L";
  const kL = lead === "L" ? PILL_K_LEAD : PILL_K_TRAIL, kR = lead === "R" ? PILL_K_LEAD : PILL_K_TRAIL;
  L.k += (kL - L.k) * Math.min(1, dt * PILL_K_RATE);
  R.k += (kR - R.k) * Math.min(1, dt * PILL_K_RATE);
  L.c = 2 * Math.sqrt(L.k) * PILL_DAMPING;
  R.c = 2 * Math.sqrt(R.k) * PILL_DAMPING;
  pull(L, s.X[s.act], dt);
  pull(R, s.X[s.act] + s.Wd[s.act], dt);
  if (reduced) {
    snap(L, s.X[s.act]);
    snap(R, s.X[s.act] + s.Wd[s.act]);
  }

  // the hover pill: only on a tab that isn't active; it follows softly and fades in and out
  const hv = s.hov >= 0 && s.hov !== s.act;
  if (hv) {
    pull(HL, s.X[s.hov], dt);
    pull(HR, s.X[s.hov] + s.Wd[s.hov], dt);
    if (reduced) {
      snap(HL, s.X[s.hov]);
      snap(HR, s.X[s.hov] + s.Wd[s.hov]);
    }
  }
  pull(HO, hv ? 1 : 0, dt);

  // the beam focuses into a thread while the pill moves fast, and blooms with a pulse when it opens up again
  const speed = Math.abs(L.v) + Math.abs(R.v);
  const fT = speed > SPEED_THIN ? FOCUS_THIN : 1;
  FO.v += (FOCUS_K * (fT - FO.x) - FOCUS_C * FO.v) * dt;
  FO.x += FO.v * dt;
  const fo = clamp(FO.x, 0, FOCUS_MAX);
  if (fo > BLOOM && !s.bloomed) {
    s.bloomed = true;
    s.pulse = 1;
  }
  if (fo < REARM) s.bloomed = false;
  s.pulse *= Math.pow(PULSE_DECAY, dt);

  // it follows the pill's centre with its own lag, leaning into the lag
  const w = R.x - L.x;
  const midT = barLeft() + L.x + w / 2;
  s.BM += (midT - s.BM) * Math.min(1, dt * FOLLOW_RATE);
  s.SK += ((midT - s.BM) * TILT - s.SK) * Math.min(1, dt * TILT_RATE);
  if (reduced) {
    s.BM = midT;
    s.SK = 0;
  }

  // tabs light up while active, and fade back when the pill leaves
  s.lit.forEach((l, i) => {
    s.lit[i] = l + ((i === s.act ? 1 : 0) - l) * Math.min(1, dt * LIT_RATE);
  });
  return { w, fo };
}

// ---- Frame ----
// Writes the frame to the DOM. Positions and sizes go out as unitless custom properties in design px (the CSS
// multiplies them by --u); opacity, colours, the beam's tilt and the canvas are written directly. Every write is
// skipped when the value hasn't changed.
const scene = document.querySelector(".scene");
const part = (name) => scene.querySelector(`[data-part="${name}"]`);
```

### M1 · Input
- On each tab `i`: `pointerenter` sets `s.hov = i` and calls `touch(performance.now())`; `pointerleave` sets `s.hov =
  -1`; `click` calls `select(i)` and then `touch(performance.now())`. (The tabs are `<button>`s, so Enter and Space
  click them.)
- On the tab list, `keydown`: with `n = tabs.length`, `from = tabs.indexOf(document.activeElement)` and `at = from < 0 ?
  s.act : from`, the target is `(at + 1) % n` for `ArrowRight`, `(at + n − 1) % n` for `ArrowLeft`, `0` for `Home`,
  `n − 1` for `End`, and none for any other key. If there's a target and none of Alt, Ctrl or Meta is down:
  `preventDefault()`, `select(target)` and `tabs[target].focus()`. (Focus follows the arrow keys; the active tab
  changes with it.)
- On `window`, `keydown` (any key, also the arrows above): `if (s.demo) s.hov = -1` (the demo's hover goes with its
  cursor), then `touch(performance.now())`.

### M2 · Start
Wait for Geist Medium, then start the loop: `const fontReady = document.fonts ? Promise.race([document.fonts.load(FONT),
new Promise((r) => setTimeout(r, FONT_WAIT))]) : Promise.resolve()`, then `fontReady.catch(() => {}).then(() => { raf =
requestAnimationFrame(frame); })`. Nothing else starts the loop. `frame` measures the tabs on its first call and keeps
asking for the next frame itself.

### The beam, frame by frame (what `drawBeam` draws)
The canvas is cleared and redrawn every frame from `T`, the pill's width `w`, the focus `f` (clamped to 0 … 1) and the
pulse. The cone's top half-width is `(4 + 6f) / 2` and its foot `lerp(14, 0.95 w, f) / 2`, so at `f` = 1 it spans 95%
of the pill and at low `f` it's a 14-px sliver. Drawn additively (`lighter`): the **body** (a trapezoid with a vertical
gradient: 0 at the top, 25% of its alpha at 45%, full at 92%, 120% at the foot; alpha 0.07 + 0.05 pulse), nine
**rays** spread across 36% of the foot, each a thin wedge from near the centre at the top, its alpha breathing
`0.55 + 0.45 sin(T s + p)`, scaled by `f`, plus 0.1 pulse at the foot; and the **core thread** (half-width 1 at the top
to `lerp(3, 0.14 × foot, f)` at the bottom, alpha 0.16 + 0.12 (1 − f) + 0.15 pulse), which is what stays when the beam
is focused. Then normally (`source-over`) 46 **dust** motes rise through the cone, drifting ±3 px sideways, brighter
towards the foot and twinkling, scaled by `f`.

### Reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`)
Part 1 reads it every frame, so switching it live takes effect at once: no demo (the cursor never shows; the bar waits
for the user), the active pill, the hover pill and the beam jump to the chosen tab instead of travelling, so the beam
never thins or blooms, and the rays and dust hold still where they were. The labels' and icons' light and the hover
pill's opacity still fade.
